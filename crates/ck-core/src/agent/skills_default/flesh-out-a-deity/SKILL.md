---
name: Flesh out a deity
description: Worldbuilding questions for a god or pantheon — domains, worship, clergy, myths, schisms. Use when the user wants to develop or deepen a deity, religion, or pantheon page.
kinds: [lore]
---

Help the user deepen a page about a deity, religion, or pantheon. Read the page first
(read_page) and use search_pages for the other gods and faiths already in the world so the
pantheon holds together — never re-ask what the page answers.

How to work:
- Pick the 2–4 questions below the page is most silent on and that matter most for play.
  Do not dump the whole list.
- These are prompts, not a form. Ask, let the user answer in their own words, then offer
  to write it into the page (you draft, they approve). Always skippable.
- Religion is lived practice before it is theology. Favour what a farmer, a soldier, or a
  priest actually does and fears over cosmology essays.
- Link related gods, cults, and holy sites (`rival: "[[...]]"`, `worshipped_in:`,
  `serves:`). If the faith has an organised church, suggest it may deserve its own
  faction page rather than crowding this one.

Questions to draw from:

**Place in the pantheon**
- Is this god one of many, the only one, or a rising upstart? Who are their kin and enemies?
- Which domains do they hold, and which do they contest with another god?
- Was there a war, a theft, or a betrayal among the gods that shaped the current order?

**Nature and symbols**
- What is the god like — stern, capricious, absent, intimate? How do they answer prayer, if at all?
- What are their symbols, sacred animals, colours, and taboos?

**Worship**
- Who prays to them, and when — harvest, battle, childbirth, death, a bargain?
- What do common people actually do: a gesture, an offering, a phrase?
- Where are the holy sites, and what makes them holy?

**Clergy and institutions**
- How is the priesthood chosen, ranked, and funded? Do they hold land or political power?
- What do the clergy say publicly versus argue about in private?

**Schisms and heresies**
- Where do believers disagree — over doctrine, a prophet, a lost text, a reform?
- Is there a persecuted sect, a splinter church, or a rival reading of the same myth?

**Truth and play**
- Is the god real, absent, or something other than worshippers believe? (Consider a
  `[!secret]` callout for the truth.)
- What do miracles and curses look like, and what do they cost?
- How would the party meet this faith — as ally, obstacle, or a debt they did not know they carried?
